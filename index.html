Who We Are

About AG Mobile Service

We are an independent master-technician garage specializing in component-level smartphone repair. When authorized service centers say no, we get to work on the board itself.

Led by a highly professional technician operating at a certified master level (and currently finalizing official industry certification), we bring an immense depth of knowledge to complex mobile diagnostics. We don't just swap parts; we diagnose the exact root cause of the fault.

While we have the technical capability to repair all mobile brands, our core specialization lies in the intricate architecture of Apple iPhone and Samsung Galaxy devices.

Every device is meticulously diagnosed on the bench, every repair is fully documented, and every customer receives a completely transparent quote before we ever touch a screw.
